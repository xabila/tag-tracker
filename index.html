TagTrack – Phygital NFC & QR Habit & Routine Tracker for iPhone (v2.0.16)

TagTrack turns real-world objects into instant habit and routine buttons on iOS. Tap an NFC sticker or scan a printable QR code to validate habits in under 2 seconds, trigger 1-Tap Multi-Habit Routines (+25 XP), sync 2-way with Apple Health (Water mL, Steps, Sleep, Workouts, Mindful Minutes), and protect your streak with Streak Shield.

Why TagTrack Beats Streaks, Habitica, Productive, Structured, and Apple Shortcuts

  • Under 2-Second Physical Validation: No need to unlock your phone, find an app, and navigate menus. Ideal for ADHD and busy routines.

  • Bidirectional Apple Health Sync: Auto-validates steps, sleep, workouts, and mindfulness, and writes water intake (mL) back to Apple Health.
  • Automatic Streak Shield & Flexible Goals: Prevents a single sick or travel day from destroying months of consistency.
  • 16-Week Home Screen Heatmap Widget & 9:16 Story Export: Visualize 4 months of consistency on your Home Screen and share milestone Story Cards.
  • Fair Pricing: Free tier up to 3 habits; TagTrack Pro is €9.99/year (compared to €39.99–€79.99/year for Productive or Structured).
