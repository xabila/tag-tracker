How does TagTrack work?

Building habits is hard. Logging them shouldn't be. Three steps to zero-friction routines.

  1. 1

    Pick your habit or routine

    Choose from 50+ presets across Health, Fitness, Mindfulness, and Home — or create your own daily, weekly, or monthly goal.

  2. 2

    Link an NFC Tag or QR Code

    Hold your iPhone near any NFC sticker to write it in 1 second, or print a ready-to-stick QR code sheet.

  3. 3

    Tap the real object to log

    Touch your water bottle, pill box, or gym bag with your iPhone. Your habit and Apple Health update in under 2 seconds.
